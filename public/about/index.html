Purpose of the Dimo app

The purpose of Dimo is to help people track personal spending. Dimo is a personal expense tracker. It lets users log everyday expenses, set category budgets, manage recurring bills, review spend stats, and optionally get purchase suggestions from Gmail on iOS.

Dimo is built for individual personal use. It is not a bank, payment processor, tax advisor, or advertising product.

What Dimo does

  • Log expenses — amounts, categories, payment methods, notes, and dates.
  • Budgets and recurring bills — monthly limits and subscriptions in one place.
  • Stats — review spending by period, category, and merchant.
  • Lending records — track money lent or borrowed (native apps).
  • Optional Gmail suggestions (iOS) — with consent, suggest purchases or refunds to review before saving.

Why Dimo requests Google user data

Dimo requests Google account access only for the purposes below. Dimo does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

  • Sign-in (name, email, profile photo) — to create and recognize your Dimo account so your workspace can sync across your devices. These fields are read-only in Dimo.
  • Gmail read-only (optional, iOS) — if you connect Gmail, Dimo uses gmail.readonly access to find purchase and refund emails and suggest expenses you can accept or dismiss. Gmail tokens stay on your device. You can disconnect anytime.
